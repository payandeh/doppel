# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want Doppel's community to be welcoming and harassment-free for everyone, whatever their age, body size, visible or
invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education,
socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Expected behavior

- Be kind and patient, especially with newcomers.
- Respect different viewpoints and experiences.
- Give and accept constructive feedback gracefully.
- Take responsibility for mistakes and learn from them.
- Focus on what is best for the project and its users.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Sexualized language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope and enforcement

This applies in all project spaces (issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews) and when someone represents the project
elsewhere. Report problems privately to the maintainer, [@payandeh](https://github.com/payandeh), through a
[private security advisory](https://github.com/payandeh/doppel/security/advisories/new) or by contacting them directly.
Reports are handled confidentially.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that break this code,
and may temporarily or permanently ban anyone whose behavior is harmful.
